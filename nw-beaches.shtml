North Oregon Coast:
Beaches, Viewpoints and Waysides

Last update: November, 12, 2026


Area C - 60-90 miles West of Beaverton

This list has some overlaps with other site boxes on the main travel page. Driving from Astoria South toward Newport on Hwy 101 (about 100+ miles), there are numerous stop points for breaks, photos or to just hike/look around. All beaches are public property and free access.

Not all beach accesses and viewpoints are listed. Just the most well known.

Need to pray for good weather. Fog in the Summer is a thing on the Oregon Coast.

South Jetty of the Mouth of the Columbia River. See the Fort Stevens State Park site box on NW Travel page.


All of the parking areas at Fort Stevens each lead to beach access.


Seaside is a decent tourist town. The main street turnaround at the beach is well known. The promenade along the beach is over a mile long.


Ecola Point. Great post card viewpoint of the beach. Winding road to get there from Hwy 101. See the Cannon Beach site box on the NW Travel page.


Cannon Beach is a folksy small town with numerous shops to attract you. See the site box on the NW Travel page.


Arcadia State Park. Decent highway wayside with beach access.


Hug Point State Park. Small park with beach access. At low tide, you can see the wagon wheel ruts in the exposed beach rock from when the beach was used as the local highway.


Oswald West State Park
Couple mile downhill hike down to the beach.


Neahkahnie Mountain
See the site box on the NW Travel page.


Manzanita. Small town in northern Tillamook County. Nice shops. Well known beach
adjacent at Nehalem Bay State Park.


Rockaway Beach. Coast town spread along Hwy 101. Lots of stores. Beach access.


Barview Jetty County Campground. From here you can walk down the north side of the Barview Jetty and entry to Tillamook Bay.


Bayocean Peninsula Park is on the south side of the Barview Jetty. Location of a former community of Bayocean. Lots of nice homes. When the state built the jetty entrance to Tillamook Bay, it completely changed the tidal impact in the area. A few years later, the entire town of Bayocean succumbed to coastal erosion and disappeared. Wikipedia. Three mile hike from the last parking lot to the jetty. This access road was the site of a lot of road damage from the 1996 flood event.


3 Capes Scenic Loop is comprised of a windy road with great beach views passing by Cape Meares (see the site box on the NW Travel page), Cape Lookout (couple mile hike to nice coastal panorama to the south) and Cape Kiwanda at Pacific City.


Neskowin is a small community of beach vacation homes in Southern Tillamook County. The beach is very nice and picturesque.


Lincoln City is a well known tourist town.
See the site box on the NW Travel page.


Depoe Bay is a small town spread along Hwy 101. Known for the smallest navigable bay in the world. Also called the "whale watch capital of the Oregon Coast".


Devil's Punchbowl is small rest area just south
of Depoe Bay. See the site box on the NW Travel page.


Beverly Beach State Park. Beach access. Known for the "ghost forest": stumps of trees that were consumed by sand preventing their decay. Periodically strong winds and storms move the sand around revealing remnants of the ancient trees that are from 1000 to 7000 years old.
