Scotland/London


We originally were considering another cruise with Viking Ocean to sail around Great Britain with excursion stops to see desired sites. But all Viking ships are exactly the same and we realized we would be reliving our Iceland-Svalbard-Norway cruise again but with different stops at an even higher price due to inflation. That's when a ground tour of Scotland started to make more sense.

After review of options, a small group CEI tour of Scotland gained our attention. We wanted to add time in Edinburgh and spend several days in London on our own (not with a larger tour group).

Here is what our future trip is starting to look like:
