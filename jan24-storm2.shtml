Our Storm Experience
SW 98th Ave, Beaverton, OR

Saturday, January 13, 2024 Ice/Snow/Wind Storm

8:20am Power goes out (for 4 days)
8:30am Tree falls on south end of our house. No injuries. 5 ft diameter at base, 115 ft long.

During the hit, I was about 20 ft away in the family room with a cup of coffee in hand. Mary was in the bathroom having just left the den.

After the hit, we found that several windows around the house had popped open due to the sudden vibration and pressure change. They were easily closed.

Red thick line is the fallen tree. The red X's are 2 other trees we are nervous about. The 2nd tree just 20 ft to the north of the fallen tree is in the same situation. Both of these trees were removed when the remaining base of the fallen tree was removed:











Peering over the front gate along the south sidewalk along the house. Surprisingly the 2 den windows did not break, but their frames are twisted. 2 of 4 furnace room windows broken out. Surprisingly, all near garage side windows are still intact. For this pic, I am standing under the end of the tree on the house (the 1 ft diameter tree is in the upper right):

Tree crushes Mary's corner den (partially open air with snow blowing in).
Hard to get a clean view inside the den due to safety hazard:


This is the closet off the family room. Spiked branch thru the roof/ceiling:


Adjacent furnace room looks like a grenade went off with the collapsed ceiling. So much fallen insulation, that when I first opened the door, I thought snow had came thru the roof holes. Furnace stopped working (along with the power being out). Adjacent garage has relatively little damage (so far). We are able to open/close the garage door with no problems.


Furnace room ceiling view:

Saturday, January 20

We finally got the roof blue tarped (but tree still is in the roof).

Wednesday, January 24

Furnace finally repaired to function. It could not be repaired to final condition due to the roof condition immediately above. You can see in the above photo that the roof was compressed/deformed by the tree causing the upper tin part of the furnace to break open. Final furnace fix happens after the roof is repaired. It was a chilly 11 days in the house until the furnace starting working again. Rotating portable space heaters are not a cure-all.

Thursday, January 25

View of trimmed tree lying on the roof before removal. 12 days after it fell!.
The earliest the tree service could respond in the midst of all the competing demand for their services:


The tree service cuts the tree at the gutter line and it is removed it from the roof.
The base of the tree remains in the backyard until they can return to remove it:


It was quite the operation on tree removal day with a crane, the tree crew and power company:


With the number of damaged homes in the area, no one is getting repaired fast. Our general contractor estimates substantial work on house repairs will be Spring timeframe if not longer.

Within a quarter mile of our house, there are 3 homes in far worse state than our house.

For scale comparision, Mary is standing at base of the fallen tree:


At the 30 inch diameter cut at the house gutter, I counted 60 rings (years). With the base
of the tree (finally exposed today, next pic), Mary counted about 80 rings.
That puts the tree 9 years older than the house (built in 1953).

February 22, 2024

We got the other 2 big fir trees finally removed along with base of the fallen tree. Next comes grinding of the stumps when the tree contractor can return. Date TBD. This cost was not covered by home owners insurance. But still needed. At least we have some peace of mind. Rather than staying up at night listening to the wind and pine cones hitting the roof.

We are feeling a little disappointed as we bought this house 3+ years ago with the big fir trees as positives.

Mary on the stump of the 2nd big fir tree in the middle of our backyard fenceline.

December 2025

After some 23 months of trying to work thru reviews of our construction estimate with insurance, they finally issued repair funds and authorization to proceed with the repairs.

July 2026

Repairs completed!
